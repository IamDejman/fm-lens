A free alternative to the FM27 In-Game Editor

The In-Game Editor is a paid add-on inside FM27. FM Lens is a separate, free app that edits the same career from its own window while FM runs, on Mac and Windows.

Download FM Lens

How FM Lens differs

  • Every feature is free, editing included.
  • It has its own window beside FM, so you search and filter the whole game without leaving your screen in FM.
  • Mass edit changes a whole list of players, staff or clubs at once.
  • Templates copy one player's profile onto others, with a preview first.
  • Freezes hold a player's attributes or your club's money.
  • Nothing changes until you press Apply. Changes lists each edit, with Undo where FM allows it.

What you can edit

  • Attributes, CA and PA, positions, traits and personality.
  • Contracts, and club money and facilities.
  • Transfers, swaps, loans and releases, made by FM itself.
  • Injuries, bans, fitness and morale.

What to know first

  • FM Lens needs Football Manager 27 from Steam.
  • Edit a copy of your save.
  • FM Lens is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sports Interactive or SEGA.
