Privacy

FM Lens and this site count use anonymously with PostHog, to see how many people use them and which features matter. No names, no values you edit, no file paths and nothing you type are sent. PostHog does not keep your IP address.

The app

The app sends these events with a random install id, the app version, your macOS version and processor, and the app language. PostHog keeps the country and city.

  • app_opened
  • fm_connected: FM's version and store
  • tab_viewed: the section, such as Players
  • edit_applied: the kind of edit, such as move_player or contract.wage, never the value
  • change_undone, players_exported, report_sent

To stop it, turn off Share anonymous usage in Settings.

This site

The site counts page views and downloads started, without cookies or stored ids. It does not keep your country or city.
