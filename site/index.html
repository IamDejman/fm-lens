FM Lens
Release notes GitHub

FM Lens is a free Mac companion for Football Manager 27.

It reads the career you have loaded and lets you search, compare and edit players, staff and clubs while FM runs.

Download for Mac

For Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

What it does

Find anyone
Search and filter players, staff and clubs. Browse nations, stadiums and officials too.
Edit your save
Attributes, CA and PA, positions, traits, personality and contracts, plus club finances and facilities.
Undo any change
Edits go into FM when you press Apply. Changes lists each one, ready to undo.
Templates
Copy a player's profile onto others, with a preview first.
Squad actions
Heal injuries, lift bans, refresh fitness and sharpness, or freeze a player's attributes.
Out of your way
Works offline and updates itself.

Get started

  1. Open the disk image and drag FM Lens onto Applications. The first time, macOS cannot check the app: open System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

  2. Save and quit FM, quit Steam, then choose Set up in FM Lens.

  3. Start FM from Steam, load a copy of your save and choose Connect.
