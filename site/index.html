FM Lens
Release notes Star on GitHub

FM Lens is a free live editor and companion for Football Manager 27 on Mac and Windows.

It reads the career you have loaded and lets you search, compare and edit players, staff and clubs in real time while FM27 runs.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

For Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and Windows 10 and 11.

What it does

Find anyone
Search and filter players, staff and clubs, plus competitions, awards, officials, nations and stadiums.
Edit your save
Attributes, CA and PA, positions, traits, personality and contracts, plus club money and facilities.
Real transfers
Transfer, swap or release players through FM itself, with fees and transfer history.
Squad actions
Heal, lift bans, refresh fitness, and max morale, cohesion and tactics for the whole team.
Game actions
Add injuries, start and end loans, sign free agents and request work permits, all done by FM.
Keep players happy
See and close promises, and clear reasons for unhappiness, named as FM names them.
Freezes that hold
Fix a player's attributes or your club's money, even through long holidays.
Templates
Copy a player's profile onto others, with a preview first.
Review every change
Edits go into FM when you press Apply. Changes lists each one, with Undo where FM allows it.
Out of your way
Works offline and updates itself.

Get started

  1. On a Mac, open the disk image and drag FM Lens onto Applications. The first time, macOS cannot check the app: open System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. Then save and quit FM, quit Steam, and choose Set up in FM Lens.

    On Windows, run the installer. If Windows protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

  2. Start FM from Steam, load a copy of your save and choose Connect.

Questions

Is FM Lens free?
Yes. Every feature is free, editing included, and it is open source under GPL-3.0.
Does it work on Mac?
Yes. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and on Windows 10 and 11. You need Football Manager 27 from Steam.
Is it the In-Game Editor?
No. The In-Game Editor is a paid add-on inside FM27. FM Lens is a separate app that edits your career in real time from outside the game.
Is it an alternative to FMRTE?
Yes. FM Lens is a real-time editor for FM27 too, and every edit is free.
