A free alternative to FMRTE for FM27

FMRTE is the best known real-time editor for Football Manager. FM Lens does the same job for FM27: it edits your career while FM runs. Every edit is free, on Mac and Windows, and the code is open source.

Download FM Lens

What FM Lens gives you

  • Free, with no licence or activation. Open source under GPL-3.0.
  • One app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and Windows 10 and 11.
  • Nothing changes until you press Apply. Changes lists each edit, with Undo where FM allows it.
  • Transfers, loans, injuries and work permits are made by FM itself.
  • Works offline and updates itself.

What you can edit

  • Attributes, CA and PA, positions, traits and personality.
  • Contracts, and club money and facilities.
  • Transfers, swaps, loans and releases.
  • Injuries, bans, fitness and morale.
  • Freezes and templates.

What to know first

  • FM Lens needs Football Manager 27 from Steam.
  • It edits the FM27 builds it has been checked against. Other builds open read-only until FM Lens updates.
  • Edit a copy of your save.

FM Lens is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sports Interactive or SEGA.
