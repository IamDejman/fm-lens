How to freeze attributes in FM27

A freeze stops a player's attributes and current ability from dropping, so he does not decline with age or after an injury.

  1. Start FM from Steam, load a copy of your save and choose Connect in FM Lens.
  2. Open Players and search for the player.
  3. Open Tools and turn on Freeze attributes.

How long it holds

A freeze holds while FM Lens is connected, even through long holidays. The next time you connect to that save, FM Lens turns your freezes back on.

Freeze a whole list

Mass edit can freeze or unfreeze every player in a list.

Freeze money too

Freeze money does the same for your club's balance and transfer budget.
