How to heal injuries in FM27

One player

  1. Start FM from Steam, load a copy of your save and choose Connect in FM Lens.
  2. Open Players, search for the player and open him.
  3. Open Medical and choose Heal beside Injuries. It heals all his injuries at once.

The whole team

  1. Open Clubs, open your club and choose Squad.
  2. Pick the team, such as First team.
  3. Choose Heal. It heals everyone in that team at once.

Bans and fitness

Beside Heal in the squad are Lift bans, Refresh fitness for full energy and match sharpness, and Remove unhappiness. For one player, choose Lift beside Bans in Medical.
