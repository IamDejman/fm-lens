How to edit club money in FM27

  1. Start FM from Steam, load a copy of your save and choose Connect in FM Lens.
  2. Open Clubs, search for the club and open it.
  3. Choose Edit. Under Finances, change Balance, Transfer budget or Total wage budget.
  4. Press Apply. Changes lists the edit, with Undo.

Keep the money from running out

While editing, tick Freeze money under Finances. The balance and transfer budget can't go down while FM Lens is connected, even through long holidays.

If a club has no Finances

FM only tracks money for clubs in playable leagues.
