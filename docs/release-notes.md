## What's new

FM Lens 0.7.0 makes your edits land the way FM's own do. Transfers, swaps and releases now go through the game itself, Intel Macs get every action that runs inside FM, promises arrive on Windows, and FM Lens speaks FM's language everywhere, with names in place of numbers. Search every result, filter every list, and trust that freezes hold through the longest holiday.

### Transfers FM really makes

- Transfer a player to another club and FM makes it a real transfer: the fee is paid, and both clubs' Transfers In and Out lists show the deal, just like a signing you made in the game.
- Transfer players from any club, not only your own.
- Swap two players and FM records two transfers, one each way, with the players' contracts kept.
- Release a player and FM ends his contract the way its own editor does: he becomes a free agent at once.
- Transfers, swaps and releases are now permanent, as in FM. The dialogs tell you before you confirm, and Changes shows no Undo for them.
- Works on Apple silicon, Intel Macs and Windows.
- Cancel a transfer and the signing it came from no longer offers an Undo that could not work.

### Every game action on Intel Macs

- Intel Macs now get every action that runs inside FM, the same as Apple silicon: add and heal injuries, start and end loans, sign free agents, transfers and future transfers, releases, competitions, awards and Max tactics.

### Promises on Windows

- The Mood tab on Windows lists the promises made to a player, with when each was made and when it is due, just as on a Mac.
- Close a promise and FM marks it ended at once. Changed your mind? Undo opens it again.

### FM's names, never numbers

- Promises, formations and reasons for unhappiness now use FM's own wording, so "Promise 208" reads "Preferred position and role" and every formation in the dropdowns has its name, such as 4-2-3-1 DM AM Wide.
- New reasons for unhappiness are named too, such as "Wants to start more games".
- Where FM Lens has no name for something, it says so in plain words, like Unknown club or Unknown promise, instead of showing a code.
- Changes names what you did: "Closed promise: Preferred position and role".

### Search and lists

- See every search result, not just the first 200. Each list pages through 200 at a time with Previous and Next.
- A new search clears the old results straight away and shows Searching, so you never act on rows from the last search. If a search fails, Retry is right there.
- Competitions, Awards, Officials, Nations and Stadiums now have Filters, like Players, Staff and Clubs: men or women, nation, continent, reputation, prestige, age, ability and capacity.
- Stadiums list each ground once, with every club that plays there, so shared grounds like Stadio Olimpico show both clubs together.
- The Nations list now includes nations that only have staff or competitions.
- Change the language or currency and you stay where you were: same page, same open profile, and lists you have not opened yet remember their search.
- At 125% and 150% zoom, player names stay readable: the less important columns make room first.

### Edits you can trust

- Type a wage and that exact wage is applied. £260,000 stays £260,000, and FM Lens shows FM's rounded weekly figure next to it when the two differ.
- Grant work permit is now Request permit. When FM decides the player is not eligible and issues nothing, FM Lens tells you so instead of showing an error.
- Half-typed entries count as unsaved changes. If something is not valid yet, FM Lens asks before you leave the page or close the dialog, instead of dropping it.
- Max cohesion, Unsackable and Register squad any time have been checked in FM and are no longer marked experimental.
- On Windows, an edited official shows the change in FM straight away.

### Freezes that hold

- Freezes now hold through long holidays. When FM reshuffles its lists, FM Lens reads the game again by itself and keeps every hold, so a frozen club's money and frozen attributes stay put.
- FM Lens checks the game every few seconds and picks up each new day on its own, unless you have changes waiting.
- Staff freezes are remembered after FM Lens restarts, and listed when they come back on.
- Staff profiles show when a freeze is on, and turning a freeze off clears the "back on" notice.
- Picking another job clears the message from a job change FM refused.

### Faster and smoother

- FM Lens opens lighter: a much smaller logo, and each list loads the first time you open it, then keeps its search and selection as you move around.
- With Settings open, Cmd+1 to Cmd+9 no longer switch the page behind it.
- The Windows installer shows the FM Lens icon and wordmark.
