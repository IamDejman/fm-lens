## What's new

FM Lens 0.7.4 lets you show off what you find in your save. Turn any player into a share card in two clicks, see the transfer value FM keeps for him, and keep editing deep into a long career.

### Share any player

- A new Share button in every player profile makes a card of that player, ready for X, Reddit or your group chat.
- Pick a Post (1080 by 1350) or a Story (1080 by 1920).
- The card shows the player's top 8 attributes, his age, value, wage and the in-game date. Show or hide CA and PA with one switch.
- Copy image puts the card straight on your clipboard, Save PNG keeps it in Documents, FM Lens, Exports, and Copy caption gives you a line to post with it.
- The card is drawn on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

### Base transfer value

- The Contract tab now shows a player's base transfer value beside Asking price, read straight from the game.

### Long careers

- Long careers stay editable. Players born after 2020 no longer make FM Lens lock players and staff as read-only, so newgens in the 2030s and beyond are fine.
- Set a date of birth for players and officials up to the game's own date, not only up to 2020.

### Problem reports

- When FM Lens locks part of the game, Report a problem now sends which checks did not pass, so the problem can be found and fixed faster.

### Your email

- FM Lens asks for your email address once on each computer, before it opens, so it can tell you about new versions.
- The address goes to the FM Lens mailing list and nowhere else. Kit sends one email to confirm it. No captcha, no browser page.

### Checked on every machine

- The share card, base transfer value, birth dates and email screen were each checked in FM27 27.0.1 on an Apple silicon Mac, an Intel Mac and a Windows PC before this release.

### Updating

- Open FM Lens and it fetches 0.7.4 by itself. Press Relaunch to update when it appears, or download 0.7.4 from getfmlens.com.
