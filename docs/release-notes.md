## What's new

FM Lens 0.7.7 makes mood edits last. Remove unhappiness, press Continue, and your squad is still happy the next day. And Mass edit can now freeze everything in a list in one go.

### Mood edits that stay

- Remove unhappiness now ends a player's reasons the way the game's own action does, so the same complaints do not come back a day later. It works from a player's Mood tab and as a squad action.
- No more "Squad unhappy with club situation" the morning after you fixed it. Edit morale or happiness and the game works out the dressing room atmosphere, and how much the squad backs you, again from how the players feel now.
- Set a player's happiness and it is still there after Continue. FM Lens moves his morale to match and ends the reasons that would drag it back, and each shows in Changes so you can undo it.
- A mass edit of happiness does the same for every player in the list.
- Remove unhappiness now reaches players on loan too.
- Undo still puts everything back as it was.

### Freeze all in Mass edit

- Mass edit has the profile's freeze box: Freeze attributes for players and staff, Freeze money for clubs.
- Tick it and press Apply, and everything your search and filters select is frozen at once. Your whole squad, every wonderkid on a shortlist, a league's worth of clubs: one Apply.
- Untick it and press Apply to unfreeze them all. When only some of the list is frozen, the box sits in between and a click freezes the rest.
- Fill in fields as well and the new values are written first and frozen after, so what you set is what is held.
- The preview counts the freeze beside the changes, and Undo this mass edit takes the freeze back with them.
- The freeze is remembered with your save, so it is back on the next time you connect.
- This one came from a player's request. Keep them coming on the Wishlist.

### Updating

- Open FM Lens and it fetches 0.7.7 by itself. Press Relaunch to update when it appears, or download 0.7.7 from getfmlens.com.
