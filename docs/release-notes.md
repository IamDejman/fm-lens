## Install

Open **FM-Lens-0.5.4-macos-universal.dmg** and drag **FM Lens** onto **Applications**. The first time you open it, go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.

It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, with Football Manager 27 version 27.0.0 from Steam. Other versions open read-only.

## Update

Already have FM Lens 0.5.1 or later? It updates itself. On 0.5.0, drag this one onto **Applications** and choose **Replace**.

## What's new

- Change how long an injury lasts, or take one injury out.
- See and change a manager's formations in and out of possession. They show as numbers for now.
- See and change a nation's style ratings.
- See a competition's rules.
- See a player's promises, and close an open one.
- Change club: become the manager of another club.
- The version shows at the foot of the window.
- FM Lens looks for updates every four hours while it's open.

## Known issues

- FM shows most changes after the next day, or after you reload the screen.
- FM raises a lowered level in the main language of a club's country again within a few days.
- Cancelling an agreed transfer can't be undone.
- Not every field has been tested through a save and reload.
