## What's new

FM Lens 1.1.2 fixes the scout card when FM is full screen on a Mac, and lets you move players out of your own club on every FM27 version you can edit. Everything below is new since 1.1.1.

### Scout card on a full screen FM (macOS)

- Rest the pointer on a player, staff or club name in FM and FM Lens's card appears beside it. On a Mac that only worked with FM in a window: with FM full screen the card never showed. It now shows either way, so you can keep FM full screen and still see ability, potential and the rest at a glance.
- Thank you to the player who reported it from the app.

### Move players out of your own club

- Transfer, swap or release a player of the club you manage, on every FM27 version FM Lens can edit. Where FM Lens can ask FM to make the transfer, that already worked. Where FM Lens makes the move itself, it used to stop with "He plays for your club. Transfer him in FM." That is gone: he moves like any other player.
- He keeps his wage and contract length, and joins the new club on today's date in your game.
- We played it through on a test career: FM marks his place in your tactics as empty, your assistant picks someone else, and matches, saves and loads carry on as normal. Check your tactics and set pieces after you move a starter.
- This came from a wish sent in the app. Keep them coming: Wishlist is in the menu.

### Updating

- Open FM Lens and it fetches 1.1.2 by itself. Press Relaunch to update when it appears, or download 1.1.2 from getfmlens.com.

FM Lens is free and open source, and it will stay that way. A [star on GitHub](https://github.com/IamDejman/fm-lens) encourages me.
