## What's new

FM Lens 0.7.3 is ready for Football Manager 27's first patch. FM27 updated to 27.0.1 on 10 November, and until now FM Lens could only read the patched game. With 0.7.3 every edit and every action works again, on Mac and on Windows.

### Works with FM27 27.0.1

- Edit on the patched game again: player and staff attributes, contracts and wages, club money and nations all save as they did before the patch.
- The actions FM carries out for you are back too: Transfer, Loan and End loan, Release, Sign now and Sign later, Cancel transfer, work permits, Add injury and Heal, Lift bans, Refresh fitness, Remove unhappiness and Max tactics.
- Undo in Changes puts a value back on 27.0.1 just as it did before.
- The "Read-only: this FM version isn't supported yet" notice is gone on 27.0.1. You no longer have to press Edit anyway.
- Your edits stay in your save. Save the career, close FM, open it again and they are still there.

### Checked in the game on every machine

- Every edit and action above was run in FM27 27.0.1 on an Apple silicon Mac, in FM's Intel Mac version and on a Windows PC before this release, and FM kept running through all of them.
- Still on 27.0.0? FM Lens 0.7.3 works with it as well, so you can update FM Lens first and the game whenever Steam gets to it.

### Updating

- Open FM Lens and it fetches 0.7.3 by itself. Press Relaunch to update when it appears, or download 0.7.3 from getfmlens.com.
