## What's new

FM Lens 0.7.6 shows who works for which team. A club's first team, U21s and U18s each have their own manager and coaches, and the Staff tab now keeps them apart.

### Staff by team

- The club Staff tab has the Squad tab's team switch: First team, U21, U18 or B team, whichever teams the club has.
- Each team lists its own staff, its manager first, then the coaches and the rest of the backroom.
- No more three Managers in one list: the first-team, U21 and U18 managers each sit with their own team.
- Move a member of staff to another club and they join its first team. Undo puts them back with their old team.

### Updating

- Open FM Lens and it fetches 0.7.6 by itself. Press Relaunch to update when it appears, or download 0.7.6 from getfmlens.com.

FM Lens is free and open source, and it will stay that way. A [star on GitHub](https://github.com/IamDejman/fm-lens) encourages me.
