## What's new

FM Lens 0.7.1 polishes the things you touch every day. Young signings go straight into your senior squad, dates type the way you expect on every machine, a new calendar follows the game's date, and a long list of small snags in profiles, lists and dialogs is gone.

### Signings that land right

- Sign or transfer a young player and he joins your senior squad, not the U21s.
- Sign free agents who can also coach. Before, Sign was offered and then failed.
- Players who hold a coaching job at a club no longer show Transfer or Release, since FM moves them as staff.
- A player signed with Sign later may still start in the U21s, because FM makes that signing on its day.

### Dates and a new calendar

- Type any date as plain digits: 20072028 becomes 20/07/2028, and the separators write themselves. On a Mac this used to give the wrong date.
- Dates are typed in the order of your language, with dots in Turkish.
- Click into a date and all of it is selected, so typing replaces it.
- A new calendar, the same on Mac and Windows. It opens on the field's date, or on the game's date, with the game day ringed.
- Move through the calendar with the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down. Escape closes it and leaves the dialog open.
- Keep typing while the calendar is open and the digits go to the date. On Windows they used to be ignored.
- The "Contract ends by" filter starts from the game's date, not today's real date.
- Screen readers can read every day and button of the calendar.

### Work permits

- The Work permit section now asks FM's own rules. Where a player needs no permit for his club's country it reads Not needed, and you are no longer offered a request FM would turn down.
- Revoke works for a player on loan whose permit is for his parent club's country, and Undo gives it back.

### Wages and ability

- When FM rounds a wage, the profile says so: £260,000 shows "FM shows £250,000" under it, for players and staff.
- Max attributes, and Set all attributes to 20, now raise CA and PA to 200 too, so FM shows the new ability straight away. One Undo puts everything back.

### Changes that say what you did

- Squad actions are named in Changes: Lift bans, Heal, Refresh fitness and the rest, with the club, and the team when it is not the first team. Undo all takes the whole action back.
- The Changes count leaves out entries from an earlier game, which can no longer be undone.

### Profiles, lists and dialogs

- In a profile, Edit reads Done while you are editing, and pressing it ends the editing.
- The profile header keeps its shape: the name stays on one line and the status chips scroll, so the tabs never jump.
- A club's Finances show a negative transfer budget, in red, instead of hiding.
- A national team's manager is a Manager at that team in the list, the profile and the job filters, not Unemployed.
- Club pickers list men's clubs first for a man and women's clubs first for a woman.
- In Squad, click a row or a name to open that player in the player list, with the filters cleared.
- More closes once you are done with Export, Mass edit or Clone & templates.
- Templates: Save keeps the clone's result and its Undo, the picker shows the template in use, and Save and Delete confirm right under their buttons.

### Getting connected

- When connecting to FM fails, FM Lens now counts the reason as a short code, never names, files or paths, so we can fix what stops people getting started. The privacy page on getfmlens.com lists it.
