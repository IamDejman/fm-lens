## What's new

FM Lens 1.1.3 brings editing back after Football Manager 27's 27.1.0 update on Steam, on Mac and on Windows, the same day the update landed. Everything below is new since 1.1.2.

### FM27 27.1.0 on Steam: editing is back

- Steam updated FM27 to version 27.1.0 on 17 November. The update moved everything FM Lens relies on inside the game, so FM Lens could only read your career. FM Lens 1.1.3 knows this version, and every edit and action works again.
- That covers the edits FM Lens makes itself, such as attributes, CA and PA, contracts, club money, staff and nations, and the moves FM makes for FM Lens: transfers, loans, signing free agents now or for a later date, releases, injuries and healing, work permits, lifting bans, refreshing fitness, removing unhappiness and Max tactics. Undo in Changes puts any of them back.
- Club finances read properly on 27.1.0. The update shifted where FM keeps part of a club's finance record, which would have left every club's balance and budgets blank. FM Lens 1.1.3 reads them from the right place.
- We played it through before release on Apple Silicon Macs, Intel Macs and Windows: every kind of edit above, then a save, a full restart of FM and a reload, with every change still in place.
- Earlier versions keep working: 27.0.1 and 27.0.0 on Steam, and Game Pass version 0.9.2435.0.

### Updating

- Open FM Lens and it fetches 1.1.3 by itself. Press Relaunch to update when it appears, or download 1.1.3 from getfmlens.com.
- As always, edit a copy of your save.

FM Lens is free and open source, and it will stay that way. A [star on GitHub](https://github.com/IamDejman/fm-lens) encourages me.
