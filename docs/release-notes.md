## What's new

FM Lens 1.0 is here, and it is the biggest release yet. FM Lens now scouts as well as it edits: every player has a Scout tab, lists rank by what people are best for, and a scout card shows over the game itself, on Windows and the Macs. The lists do far more, position edits finally stay, and there is a long run of new things to read and edit. Everything below is new since 0.7.10.

### Scouting

- Every player has a Scout tab. It shows his best roles today and at his peak, his chance of developing and how much he is expected to grow in two years. These are FM Lens's own estimates, worked out from what the game holds.
- Find similar. Press As he is today or At his peak and the player list is ranked 0 to 100 against him, with a Like column.
- 57 role and duty ratings. Pick a role in the filters and it becomes a column you can sort.
- New filters for attributes at potential, chance of development and expected growth.
- Top players, under Lists: the best, or the most promising at 21 and under, men's or women's, across the world, a continent or a country. In the Steam version you can narrow it to a division.
- Best for. Rank players by 17 recipes such as ST: Finishing, and staff by 14 such as Coach: Attacking. A Fit column scores each out of 100, and resting the pointer on a score shows the attributes behind it. On a real save, ST: Finishing put Haaland on 89, Mbappé on 88 and Kane on 86.
- Your best. A ranked list shows your club's best beside the picker and marks everyone who beats him. A profile shows the three things that person is best for.
- Your careers remember. Each career you connect is recorded once a month of game time, on your computer only. The Scout tab shows how the same player grew in your other careers.
- FM Lens's scout card shows over FM, on Windows and the Macs. Rest the pointer on the (i) by a player's name in the game and the card shows his current and potential ability, his best three roles, his chance of development and how fast he is progressing. Hold Shift for his peak. Click the card and he opens in FM Lens. Settings turns it off.

### Lists

- Columns, under More. Choose the columns of the player list and add up to eight attribute columns. Value and Wage are columns now, and each one sorts the list.
- Saved filters on every list: staff, clubs, nations, stadiums, competitions, awards and officials, as well as players.
- Filter players by several positions at once, and by a range of reputation.
- Tick rows. Tick players, staff or clubs, and Mass edit and Clone & templates act on the ones you ticked. Ticks stay through searches, filters, sorting and pages.
- Every nation and every named stadium in the game is listed, not only the ones your save uses.
- Club logos, faces, flags, competition logos and kits now come from FM's own files, with no pack to install. Kits from your own kit packs show on a club's Overview.
- Search is much faster on large databases.
- FM Lens reopens where you left off: the section, each list's search, filters, sort, page and scroll, and the open profile and its tab. It keeps this for each career.
- Lists and profiles keep their scroll position when you leave and come back, and after Apply and Undo.
- Mass edit, Clone & templates and Export work on the players the list shows, in its order.

### Editing

- Position edits stay. FM used to put the old ratings back within days. Now they hold: we checked through 13 days of holiday and three rounds of save, quit and relaunch. Undo puts the old rating back exactly.
- Remove unhappiness goes all the way. It ends a player's reasons to be unhappy, sets his morale to 20 and his happiness and playing time happiness to 100, and takes his manager off the people he dislikes. One Undo takes it all back. It works on one player, in a mass edit and on a whole team.
- Inspire, on the Mood tab, takes a player's morale to 20 and removes his unhappiness in one press.
- Editing morale, happiness or playing time happiness now changes only that value.
- Drag a number to change it. While you edit a profile, drag an input right for more and left for less. Money moves in round steps, wages in the steps FM shows, and nothing is written until you press Apply.
- Contract dates. Edit the day a contract was signed and the day its holder joined, for players and staff.
- Staff tendencies. A staff profile lists his tendencies under FM's own names, strongest first, and you can set each from 1 to 20.
- Referee attributes. An official's profile lists seven, from Allowing Flow to Time Keeping, and each can be set from 1 to 20.
- Women's FIFA points. A nation's profile shows the women's world ranking and points, and you can edit the points.
- A money freeze now holds a club's transfer budget as well as its balance.
- A player's Bonuses and clauses now lists his competition bonuses by name.

### More to read and edit

These are in the Steam version, on Windows and the Macs. They are not on Game Pass yet.

- Instalments and buy-backs. A player's Contract tab lists the instalments still owed for him and the buy-back clause on him. Change the monthly amount, the months left, the buy-back fee and the days it runs between, each with its own Undo.
- Add a like or dislike. Favoured and disliked people and clubs each have an Add button: search, pick, done.
- Add a language to a player or a member of staff. FM's Personal page lists it at once.
- Swap seat. A board member can swap seats with another, on the same board or at another club, and each takes the other's job and wage. Undo puts both back.
- End loan now works on a loan with a future fee. The fee is dropped and he goes back to his own club.
- The header shows the name of the save FM has loaded, and follows Save As and Load.
- A player's Contract tab shows whether he is registered in the league squad, and his award bonuses. His Career tab shows the clubs, nations and competitions the game has him aiming for.
- A staff member's Career tab shows his scouting knowledge of each nation, 0 to 100.
- A club's Overview shows its ownership type, its income by source for this season and last, and the colours of its home, away and third kits.
- A competition's rules show the substitutions allowed and the substitutes named this season.

### Smoother

- When FM loads another save, FM Lens reads the game again by itself, and the nation picker shows the right nations afterwards.
- An Undo that is refused now says why in the values you see, and tells you when a later change of yours has to be undone first.
- On a game version FM Lens can't edit yet, the notice now offers Check for updates, then Relaunch to update.
- The More panel closes as its dialogs open, and the Stadiums capacity filter has room for 80,000.
- Support FM Lens in the app menu opens the Say thanks window again.

### Your privacy

- FM Lens now records how its own window is used, to find what is hard to use. What you type, your manager's name, email addresses and your folder names are hidden, and recordings are kept for 30 days. It is on by default. Turn it off in Settings with Share usage and recordings of this window.

### Updating

- Open FM Lens and it fetches 1.0.0 by itself. Press Relaunch to update when it appears, or download 1.0.0 from getfmlens.com.

FM Lens is free and open source, and it will stay that way. A [star on GitHub](https://github.com/IamDejman/fm-lens) encourages me.
