## What's new

FM Lens 0.7.10 makes attribute edits stay. Raise a player's attributes and his current ability now rises with them, so the game no longer pulls them back when the month turns. And if FM Lens has made your save better, you can now say thanks without leaving the app.

### Edits that stay

- You told us that attributes you raised came back down after you played on. They did, and here is why: once a month FM fits every attribute to the player's current ability. Raise the attributes alone and that number is left behind, so the game scales them all back to it.
- We reproduced it on a goalkeeper with CA 168. Eight attributes were set to 20 and CA was left alone. They held for a week, then at the turn of the month all eight read 18, and attributes nobody had touched fell 1 to 2 as well.
- Now, while you edit attributes or positions, FM Lens fills in the CA they are worth as you type. Apply sends the attributes and the CA together, so the game has nothing to scale back.
- A potential below the new CA is raised to match it. A potential set as a range is left alone.
- You stay in charge. A CA you type yourself is kept as typed. When your edits are worth less than the CA he has, nothing moves until you press Suggested CA.
- Type an attribute back to what it was and the CA goes back too.
- For a player you raised before this update, open him, press Edit and press Suggested CA before the month ends. His attributes then hold.

### Say thanks

- FM Lens is free and it stays free. If you want to support it, Support FM Lens now sits at the foot of the window, in Settings under About, and in the Help menu on Mac.
- It opens a Say thanks window with my Ko-fi page inside the app. Tip once or monthly, any amount, with a note if you like.
- Open in browser takes the same page to your browser if you would rather pay there.
- Ko-fi handles the payment. FM Lens sees none of it, and nothing about your save is sent.
- It comes in English, Spanish and Turkish.

### Updating

- Open FM Lens and it fetches 0.7.10 by itself. Press Relaunch to update when it appears, or download 0.7.10 from getfmlens.com.

FM Lens is free and open source, and it will stay that way. A [star on GitHub](https://github.com/IamDejman/fm-lens) encourages me.
