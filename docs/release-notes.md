## What's new

FM Lens 0.7.5 opens up every club's backroom and gives you a direct line to what gets built next. See who runs a club from the manager down to the board, and ask for the next feature without leaving the app.

### A club's staff

- Club profiles have a new Staff tab: everyone on the club's staff, from the manager and coaches to recruitment, the medical team and the board.
- Each job is grouped together, the best first, with job, age, nation, CA and PA.
- Click anyone to open them in the staff list, with its filters cleared so they are always there.

### Wishlist, in the app

- A Wishlist button at the foot of the window asks what FM Lens should do next. Write it and press Send.
- Add screenshots or videos if they help: attach, paste or drop them on the window.
- Your wish is posted publicly on GitHub as a feature request, with your FM Lens version and nothing else. No account, no browser.

### Your email

- Your address now stays on FM Lens's own list, and FM Lens sends its emails from getfmlens.com. No mailing service in between.
- No email to confirm it: the address is on the list as soon as you press Continue.
- FM Lens asks for it once, in the app. The download page on getfmlens.com no longer asks.

### A star on GitHub

- After a few edits, a small notice under the masthead asks for a star on GitHub. Star on GitHub or Not now, and it never comes back.
- Star on GitHub is also in Settings, About.

### Updating

- Open FM Lens and it fetches 0.7.5 by itself. Press Relaunch to update when it appears, or download 0.7.5 from getfmlens.com.

FM Lens is free and open source, and it will stay that way. A [star on GitHub](https://github.com/IamDejman/fm-lens) encourages me.
