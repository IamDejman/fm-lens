## What's new

FM Lens 0.6.1 is the biggest redesign yet: a player profile in tabs, a proper Settings window, filters on every list and a tighter layout that fits more on screen. FM Lens now uses FM's own names for values it used to show as numbers, warns you before a signing cancels a deal, and has passed its first full test run on Windows.

### A new player profile

- Five tabs: Attributes, Mood, Contract, Medical and Career. No more scrolling a long page to find a clause or an injury.
- Status chips under the name show at a glance if a player is injured, banned, unhappy or near the end of his contract. Click a chip to jump to its tab.
- Heal, Lift, Change, Grant and Prevent retirement sit on the headings of the sections they act on. Transfer and Release sit at the end of the chips.
- Position ratings are drawn on the pitch, where you would look for them.
- Medical lists injuries and bans as rows, and unhappiness moves to Mood with the rest of his feelings.
- Full name, birth date, height and ID sit together under Personal details, and you can change them in Edit mode.
- FM Lens remembers your tab as you go from player to player.

### Staff, clubs and competitions

- Staff profiles get tabs too: Attributes, Contract and Career. Attributes use two columns when the panel is narrow.
- Your club is one click away: its name in the top bar opens Clubs on your squad.
- The club overview shows the wage budget used per week, and long club names are no longer cut off.
- Click a competition to see its short name and rules right under the row. Click again or press Escape to close them.

### Lists and filters

- Players, staff, clubs and stadiums share one Filters button. Each filter in effect shows as a chip you can remove, and Clear removes them all.
- Export, Mass edit and Clone & templates sit behind one More button, which leaves more room for the list.
- With a profile open, the player list stays compact: name and club, positions, CA and PA.
- Squad actions show their counts on badges, such as Heal 5. Refresh all is now called Refresh fitness.
- Remove unhappiness on the squad screen now counts your unhappy players. It used to count none and stay off.

### Settings in its own window

- Click the gear or press Cmd+, to open Settings: General (language and currency), Appearance (theme and zoom), Privacy (usage) and About (version and game build).
- Change the language or currency and Settings opens again once FM Lens reloads. If you have unapplied edits, FM Lens asks first.

### FM's own names

- Seven staff jobs that showed as numbers now have FM's names: Managing Director, Director of Football, General Manager, Chief Doctor, Doctor, Head Performance Analyst and Owner.
- Assistant Managers are called Assistant Manager, as in FM, not Assistant Coach.
- Your club's intake team is called Youth Candidates, not Team 4.
- A new unhappiness reason is named: "Worried about the poor dressing room atmosphere".
- Jadedness reads Jaded or "-" in the squad table and on Medical. Hover to see the number, and Edit still takes a number.

### Safer signings

- When a player already has a move agreed, the Sign dialog tells you, for example "Replaces his move to Getafe on 13 Dec 2027". Signing him cancels that move.
- An empty contract end date stays blank instead of showing today's real-world date.

### A tighter, cleaner look

- One spacing scale everywhere, a slimmer top bar and denser squad rows, so more of your squad fits on screen.
- A field you have changed is framed in ink on yellow. Red now means only a value that cannot be applied.

### Easier to use with a keyboard or screen reader

- Widen the profile panel with a button beside Close, no dragging needed.
- Screen readers announce the row you select with the arrow keys, busy messages, notices, undo results and template messages.

### FM Lens on Windows: first full test run passed

- FM Lens now runs against FM27 on Windows. In our tests it read your career, ran the game's own actions (injuries, work permits, loans, signings, transfers, manager moves, max tactics) and applied and undid direct edits: attributes, contracts, mass edits, templates, squad actions and your manager.
- There is no Windows download yet. The installer, updates and the last few features come next.
