## What's new

- FM Lens updates keep using the helper already running in FM. Supported features keep working without restarting the game.
- Squad actions and profile tools are easier to find.
- Competitions, awards, officials and nations now have filters, just like players, staff and clubs. Narrow competitions by men's or women's, nation, continent and reputation; awards by men's or women's, nation, who wins them and prestige; officials by age, CA, PA and nation; and nations by continent.
- A stadium shared by more than one club now appears once, with every club that plays there. You can also show only grounds used by men's or women's clubs.
- The nations list now includes nations from competitions, not only those of players and staff.

An older helper may not refresh FM's screens immediately or support a newly added action. The latest helper loads the next time you start FM.
