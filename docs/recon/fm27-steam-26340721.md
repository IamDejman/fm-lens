# FM27 Steam build 26340721 (27.0.0)

Observed on 2 November 2026 with the Steam install under `D:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\Football Manager 27\`
(app 4530370), right after the update. The Steam test save was on 2 November 2026: 37,978 players, 21,638 staff and
23,606 clubs, with Arsenal as the human club.

The update is the one Game Pass got as 0.9.2409.0 (see the [notes on it](fm27-gamepass-0.9.2409.md)). It moved the
Steam build the same way.

## The binary

`game_plugin.dll` as mapped in memory:

| | 26340721 | 26227640 |
|---|---|---|
| Size of image | `0x1821C000` | `0x19E69000` |
| Link timestamp | `0x6AE565AF`, 526 s after Game Pass 0.9.2409.0 | `0x6ADFB11C` |

Its writable sections are `.shared`, which holds the entity arrays' pairs, the in-game date and the type descriptors,
then `.00cfg` and `.sdata`. The large read-write-execute section is `.sbss`.

## What moved

- **Entity arrays:** 0x2000 further, as on Game Pass:
  - players at `0x57057D0`;
  - staff at `0x5705818`;
  - clubs at `0x5705988`.

  With no profile, `calibrate` found them by scanning, and every sampled element decoded.
- **Retirement manager:** the static pointer is at `0x56F7D10`, still 0xDAC0 before the players pair. It is the only
  pointer to the one `RETIREMENT_MANAGER` object.
- **In-game date:** with a save loaded, the date beside the arrays (`+0x848`) read 1 January 1900, unset.
  - The date's own variable is at `0x56AF378`. It reads `32 6D EA 07`: day 306 of 2026, which is 2 November 2026. The
    game's UI held "2nd November 2026" at the same time.
  - It sits among the same type descriptors as on Game Pass (after `K_INCLUDE_GK_FORMATTER`).
  - The only other date of 2026 in the writable sections is the database's timestamp, at `+0x638` from the players
    pair.

With no profile, the checks the editor makes before it writes (`fmlibre-probe health`) all passed except the date's
("no date set"). So the app showed no ages, rather than wrong ones, and wrote nothing until allowed.

## Unchanged

`profiles/fm27/layout.toml` holds:
- **`fields --stats`:** every player and staff field read in range. Among the clubs, only the youth facilities read
  255 ("not set") at about 400 clubs, as before.
- **Arsenal:** Martin Ødegaard is captain and Bukayo Saka vice-captain.
- **Saka:** born 5 September 2001 (25 on the save's date), CA 181 and PA 188.
- **Messi:** 39.
- **Planned retirements:** 13, among them Cristiano Ronaldo and Karim Benzema on 30 June 2027.
