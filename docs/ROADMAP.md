# FM Lens feature status and roadmap

Baseline: 4 November 2026; support summaries reconciled 8 November 2026 against
dated evidence through Windows installer checks (#153). On 11 November 2026 the
platform evidence, the Intel note, the club money row, the editions row and the
language and injury rows were brought up to the 6 to 10 November entries in
EDITING-VALIDATION.md; no game checks ran for that, and the other rows were not
rechecked. This records the requested FM Live Editor-style scope.
It is not a claim that all features are implemented or achievable without
further data-layout and game-behavior research.

## Status meanings

- **Available**: present for use. Live verification is narrower than complete
  coverage. See PERSONAL-SCOUT.md and EDITING-VALIDATION.md.
- **Enabled, unverified**: the control is on for a known build. That particular feature has not passed in-game
  write, save, quit and reload checks on a disposable career.
- **Partial**: only some requested fields, entities or interactions are supported.
- **Foundation only**: inherited code exists, but writes are disabled and editing
  has not been verified on this user's career.
- **Missing / research**: needs implementation and, where relevant, mapping and
  simulation-behavior investigation.

## Platform evidence

Windows ships from 0.6.2. The Windows reads, game actions and bounded app
edit/undo checks are recorded in EDITING-VALIDATION.md and
[the Windows handoff](internal/WINDOWS-PARITY-HANDOFF.md). Those checks used
Windows Server 2022. Three of them saved an edited copy, quit FM and loaded it
again: on 8 November (build 26340721) a player's attributes and a contract wage
held, on 10 November (27.0.1, build 26385295) a player attribute, a contract
wage, a staff attribute, a club balance, Max tactics and a free-agent signing
held, and on 12 November (build 26577676) the same six and a work permit held.
On 14 November (build 26577676) a coaching licence, a staff language, traits
cloned onto 52 players, an added release clause and a raised wage were saved to
a copy and loaded again in the running game, without a quit; the wage showed in
the next payroll. Other Windows edits have no save and reload, and Windows 10/11
execution remains unverified. Unless a row names another platform, its in-game persistence
evidence is from macOS ARM64; mapped Windows fields do not inherit that evidence.

FM27 27.0.1 (Steam build 26385295) has profiles for Apple Silicon, Intel and
Windows. On 10 November it passed the PATCH-VALIDATION.md run on all three: read
checks, a bounded set of edits and game actions, and a save reloaded on the Mac
and on Windows. Rows that name build 26340721 (27.0.0) say where their evidence
came from; it was not repeated field by field on 27.0.1.

Steam build 26577676 (12 November, still 27.0.1) has its own three profiles and
passed the same run on all three that day. It is the verified build.

Intel action parity (#164): all 24 ARM64 call keys, the injury and database statics, the editor tables and the STARTING_LOAN defaults have Intel values, and the Intel bridge passes an indirect result place the System V way. The actions switch on through profile and helper capability discovery. Run under Rosetta in the packaged app on 9 and 10 November: Add injury and Heal, work permit Revoke and Request, Loan and End loan, Transfer, Release, Sign, Sign later and Cancel transfer, Max tactics, Max attributes and Lift bans, with their undos where they have one (the 9 November entries and the FM27 27.0.1 entry in EDITING-VALIDATION.md). No Rosetta run saved an edited career, so Intel has no edited-save persistence of its own; on 10 November it loaded the copy edited and saved on Apple Silicon and read those edits back. See [the mapping and Rosetta handoff](recon/fm27-steam-26340721-macos-x86_64.md#intel-action-parity-mapping-8-november-2026).

## Validation gates

- Editing evidence includes UI behavior, identity/value checks, readback,
  in-game inspection and persistence after save/reload on a disposable copy.
- Pointer/list changes do not prove a transfer behaves like an in-game move.
  Registration, history, finances and other linked systems must remain correct.
- A rule may display a new value while an instantiated competition still uses a
  cached rule. Test behavior at the relevant match, day or season boundary.
- Memory writes are not an atomic game transaction. Report partial outcomes and
  refuse unsafe undo after intervening changes.
