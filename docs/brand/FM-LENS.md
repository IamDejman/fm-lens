## Symbol prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a finished, original, premium logo symbol for the desktop app FM Lens, a personal Football Manager scouting and live-data companion. Asset: a standalone app logo mark, no lettering. Invent a striking, very simple fusion of an optical camera lens and a football: a bold circular lens silhouette, a football-inspired negative-space center, one memorable focal cut or aperture detail. It should feel precise, sporty, contemporary, intelligent and instantly recognizable at 32px, like a carefully crafted professional software identity. Choose a restrained high-contrast palette appropriate to football, with a vivid fresh green accent and a dark ink structural color; no gradients, shadows, glow, textures or 3D. Flat vector-like filled forms, strong optical balance, clean intentional geometry, generous transparent padding, centered square composition. Avoid stock magnifying-glass handles, literal cameras, shields, trophies, club crests, official Football Manager or Steam branding, generic AI sparkles, ornamental rings, tiny details and any words or letters. Output a crisp high-resolution PNG with genuine transparent background; the symbol should occupy about 75 percent of the square. One finished logo only, no presentation board, no mockup, no multiple variants.
