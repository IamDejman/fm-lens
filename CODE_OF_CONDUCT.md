# Code of conduct

FM Lens follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
in its issues, pull requests and every other project space.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, take criticism of your work as being about the
work, and leave out harassment, personal attacks and insults.

Report behaviour that breaks it to the maintainer,
[IamDejman](https://github.com/IamDejman), by email at the address on that GitHub
profile. Reports are kept private. The maintainer may remove comments, commits and
other contributions that break it, and may ban people who do so repeatedly.
